# 🛒Retail Sales Insights & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, understand customer purchasing behavior, and generate actionable insights.

An interactive dashboard was developed to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs), monthly profit trends, state-wise sales, payment methods, customer performance, product categories, and sub-category profitability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, quantity, profit, and average order value (AOV).
- Identify top-performing states and customers.
- Evaluate payment mode preferences and product category performance.
- Track monthly profit trends and identify loss-making periods.
- Compare sub-category profitability to support business decisions.
- Present insights through an interactive and visually appealing dashboard.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Sales Amount:** 438K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 5,615
- **Total Profit:** 37K
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 121K

### Sales and Profit Analysis
- State-wise sales comparison.
- Monthly profit trends, including positive and negative profit periods.
- Profitability analysis across product sub-categories.
- Customer-wise sales performance.

### Customer and Product Insights
- Quantity distribution by payment mode, including COD, UPI, debit card, credit card, and EMI.
- Quantity distribution across clothing, electronics, and furniture.
- Identification of leading customers and profitable product sub-categories.

### Interactive Filters
- **Quarter:** Qtr 1, Qtr 2, Qtr 3, and Qtr 4
- **State:** Filter sales by state.
- **City:** Filter sales by city.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi are among the states represented in the sales comparison.
- Clothing accounts for the largest quantity among the displayed product categories.
- Printers lead the displayed sub-categories in profit, followed by bookcases.
- Monthly profit analysis highlights both profitable and loss-making periods.
- Customer-wise analysis helps compare the sales contribution of individual customers.
- Payment mode analysis provides visibility into customer payment preferences.

*Note: Insights are based on the dashboard visualization and should be validated against the underlying dataset before making business decisions.*
